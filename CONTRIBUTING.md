# Contributing

House rules for this repo. Everything here is enforced by `build.py` where it
can be, and written down here where it cannot.

## Everything about one project lives under that project's collection

Four trees mirror each other, keyed on the same collection name. A post, its
generated cover, its drawn art, its social card and its announcement copy all
sit under the same folder, and the published URL carries it too:

```
posts/<collection>/YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.md
assets/covers/<collection>/<slug>.svg        generated by gen_covers.py
assets/art/<collection>/<slug>.{svg,png}     drawn by hand, never overwritten
social/<collection>/YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>-<network>.md
                    -> published at /blogs/<collection>/<slug>/
```

Collections are `ocm-mcp-server`, `fusion-mcp`, `ibm-fusion`,
`research-and-life`, defined in `COLLECTIONS` in `build.py`. Adding one is a
code change: add it there with a one-line description, or the build rejects
posts filed under it.

**Never put a post, a cover, a card or announcement copy at the top of its
tree.** The build fails on it, deliberately. That rule is the only thing
keeping this from decaying back into four flat piles sorted by date.

Related: when a post moves, its art and its social copy move with it, and the
URL changes. Leave the redirect at the old path (`build_redirect`) rather than
breaking a link somebody already shared.

## Prose

- **No em dashes.** They read as machine-written now, whatever the truth of the
  sentence. Use commas for appositives, a colon after a label, parentheses where
  the aside is really an aside.
- **Write the current state, not the story of getting there.** No "my first
  instinct was", no "what I would do differently", no "this taught me". A reader
  arriving from a search has none of that context and does not need it. Present
  tense, and prefer no first person in a technical post.
- **No "worth noting", "worth stating", "worth underlining".** Announcing that
  something matters instead of letting it matter is a tell.
- **Do not stack short fragments.** Three clipped sentences in a row is a
  cadence, not emphasis.
- Keep posts tight. A finished post is closer to 1,000 words than 3,000.

Nothing about how a change was produced belongs in the repo's provenance: no
`Co-Authored-By` trailer, no tool or assistant name in an author field, a
branch name or a "generated with" line. Commit messages are plain sentences
explaining what changed and why.

Naming an AI agent in the *content* is a different thing and is fine. The
evaluation posts compare Claude Code, Codex CLI and the Antigravity CLI by
name, because that is what they are about.

## Before pushing

```bash
python3 gen_covers.py && python3 build.py   # needs Python 3.12+
```

The build refuses a post filed outside a known collection, an unresolvable or
misfiled cover or card, an SVG social card (previews do not rasterise SVG), a
social file naming a post that does not exist, a duplicate slug, a slug that
collides with a collection name, and any internal link resolving to no page.
A green build is the check; there is no separate test suite.
